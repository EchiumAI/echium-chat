The problem

AI is powerful, but it stops at the chat window.

Teams can chat with a model, but turning that into something durable — an agent, documentation, an internal tool, a system integration — still means engineers, glue code, and a stack of SaaS subscriptions.

Chat is a dead end

Great answers evaporate. Nothing persists, compounds, or ships.

Building needs engineers

Agents, apps and integrations require dev time most teams don't have.

US-hosted & fragmented

European teams juggle many tools and worry where their data lives.

Why now

The window is open — briefly.

Foundation models are commoditised

Claude, Nova, Mistral, Llama, DeepSeek are all one API away. The value has moved up the stack — to orchestration, memory and delivery. That's where we sit.

Europe wants a European option

Regulation (AI Act, GDPR) and sovereignty concerns are pushing EU teams to look for alternatives to US-hosted tools. "Made in the EU" is now a buying reason.

No-code AI building is unsolved

Everyone can chat; almost no one can turn that into shipped software. The team that closes that gap owns the workflow.

The app-store toll is optional

The EU DMA + web/PWA distribution let us reach mobile users without paying Apple or Google a 15–30% cut. Direct-to-user economics.
